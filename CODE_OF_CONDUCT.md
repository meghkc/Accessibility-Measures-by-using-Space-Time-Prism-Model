# Code of Conduct

We are committed to fostering a welcoming and inclusive environment.

## Our Pledge
We pledge to make participation harassment-free for everyone regardless of age, disability status, ethnicity, gender identity, level of experience, or other characteristics.

## Standards
Examples of positive behavior:
- Using inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints
- Providing constructive feedback

Unacceptable behavior includes:
- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information

## Enforcement
Report issues via GitHub Issues or by contacting the project maintainer.

Repeated or severe violations may result in temporary or permanent bans.
